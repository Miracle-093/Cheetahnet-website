# CheetahNet content required before production launch

The staging website deliberately avoids unverified company claims and customer-facing placeholders. CheetahNet should provide and approve the following materials before the website is considered production-ready.

## Brand assets

- Official CheetahNet logo in SVG format, including the preferred horizontal lockup.
- Approved light-background and dark-background variants, if they differ.
- Brand usage guidance covering clear space, minimum size and approved colours.

No official logo file currently exists in this repository. The `CN` mark in the navigation remains a temporary placeholder until the official SVG is supplied.

## Coverage information

- Current approved service areas and neighbourhoods.
- The date on which the coverage information was verified.
- Rules for addresses that require manual confirmation.
- An approved contact or data owner responsible for future coverage updates.

## Support information

- Verified customer-support telephone, WhatsApp and email channels.
- Current operating hours and applicable time zone.
- Approved escalation route for installation, billing and service issues.

## Photography

- Rights-cleared installation photography.
- Approved images of technicians, equipment and completed installations.
- Written confirmation that CheetahNet may publish each asset on the website.
- Alt-text guidance or enough context for the project team to write accurate descriptions.

## Network performance

- Approved network-performance claims.
- Measurement methodology, sample period and geographic scope for every claim.
- Any required qualifications or fair-use wording.
- Named internal approver for future updates.

## Testimonials

- Real customer quotations without editing that changes their meaning.
- Customer name and customer type or company, where publication is approved.
- Written publication consent covering the website and related campaign use.
- Approved customer photography or logo usage permission, if applicable.

## Company and contact information

- Registered or approved business address.
- General contact telephone number and email address.
- Company registration details where legally required.
- Approved map location and public visiting instructions, if applicable.
